# Source policy

## PYQ verification

A question is listed as a verified PYQ only when it has:

1. an examination year and paper, and a question reference (the reference is stated as it appears in the paper; where repositories disagree on numbering, both numbers are recorded);
2. an answer, checked against an **official answer key** or a **named published solution**;
3. a URL that a reader can open.

The label records which of the two happened:

- `OFFICIAL_KEY` - answer confirmed against an official key or official solution set.
- `SOURCED` - answer confirmed against a named academic or reference source, not the key document itself.
- `UNVERIFIED` - cannot be checked. Never presented as a PYQ.

## Transcripts

Transcripts are auto-generated captions, retrieved in part. Rules:

- A transcript is evidence of what was said, not proof that it is correct.
- Never claim a whole video was analysed when only part of it was readable.
- Never reconstruct transcript content from memory.
- Coverage is recorded per video in `analysis.json` (`coverage_note`) and shown in the site.

## Third-party material

Question text is reproduced only in short form, with a link to the source, for study purposes. No textbook,
course material or transcript is copied wholesale into this repository.
