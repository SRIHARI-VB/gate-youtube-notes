# The 2x2 Distribution Grid

A decision aid rather than an animation: two toggles select one of the four distribution templates and show the method, the formula, its conditions and a verified example.

Verified examples: DODB 2^3 = 8; IODB C(7,2) = 21; DOIB S(4,2) = 7; IOIB partitions of 6 into at most 3 parts = 7.

Source: playlist Parts 4, 5 and 7; PYQ GATE CS 2022 Q.32.
