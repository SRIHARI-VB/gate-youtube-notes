The Mean, the Median and the Spread

Objective. Follow one data set - 2, 3, 3, 4 and 9 - while it is read four different ways. Watch the mean behave as a balance point, watch one outlier move the mean and the standard deviation while the median and the mode stay put, and then watch a shift of every value leave the spread alone while a doubling multiplies it. The point is the difference between the statistics that follow a x + b and the one that follows only |a|.
1.00x

Static explanation

Take the five values 2, 3, 3, 4 and 9. Their sum is 21, so the mean is 4.2. The mean is the balance point of the data: the signed deviations -2.2, -1.2, -1.2, -0.2 and 4.8 add up to zero, which is why a single large value can move it. Sorting the values gives 2, 3, 3, 4, 9, so the middle one is the median, 3, and the most frequent value is also 3, so the mode is 3. The mean sits to the right of both of them because the 9 is pulling it.

The standard deviation is a root-mean-square distance from the mean. The squared deviations are 4.84, 1.44, 1.44, 0.04 and 23.04, which sum to 30.8; dividing by five gives a variance of 6.16 and the square root of that is 2.4819. Notice that the 9 alone contributes 23.04 of the 30.8 - squaring is what makes the far value dominate, and that is the whole reason the standard deviation reacts sharply to outliers while the median does not.

Replace 9 by 19 and the sum becomes 31, so the mean rises to 6.2 and the variance to 41.36, giving a standard deviation of 6.4312. The sorted list is still 2, 3, 3, 4, 19, so the median is still 3, and 3 is still the most frequent value. This is the single most useful comparison in the topic: the mean and the standard deviation use the sizes of the values, the median and the mode use only ranks and counts.

Now add 10 to every value. The list becomes 12, 13, 13, 14, 29, the mean becomes 16.2 and the median 13, but the variance is still 41.36 and the standard deviation still 6.4312. Doubling every value instead gives 4, 6, 6, 8, 38, with mean 12.4, median 6, variance 165.44 - four times as large - and standard deviation 12.8624, twice as large. In one line: the mean, the median and the mode all satisfy new = a x + b, while the standard deviation satisfies new = |a| × old with no b anywhere. That asymmetry is exactly what GATE CSE 2011 Q.33 asks about.

Common misconception

Writing the standard deviation of aX + b as a × sd + b. A constant added to every value shifts every deviation by nothing, because the mean shifts with it; and a spread is a distance, so a negative multiplier changes direction but never size. For these five values, sd(2X + 100) is 4.9638, not 104.96.

Quick check

The base data has standard deviation 2.4819. What is the standard deviation of 3X - 7?

Answer

7.4458. The standard deviation is multiplied by |3| and the -7 does nothing to it: 3 × 2.4819 = 7.4458. Every value and the mean both shift by -7, so every deviation is unchanged.
