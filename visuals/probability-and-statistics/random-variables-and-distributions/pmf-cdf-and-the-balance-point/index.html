PMF, CDF and the Balance Point

Objective. Read one discrete distribution in three ways - as bars whose heights are probabilities, as a staircase whose height is the running total, and as a set of weights balanced on the mean - and see why the support of a variable never fixes its mean by itself.
1.00x

Static explanation

The distribution used here is the one from GATE CSE 2026 Set 1 Q.48. The variable X takes the values 1 to 8; the values 1, 2, 5 and 7 each carry probability one sixth, and the values 3, 4, 6 and 8 each carry one twelfth. Four sixths plus four twelfths is two thirds plus one third, so the table totals exactly one - the first check on any distribution.

The probability mass function is that table drawn as bars. Its cumulative version, the CDF, is F(x) = P(X is at most x), a staircase that is flat between values and jumps at each value by exactly that value's probability. At x = 4 the staircase has climbed through one sixth, one sixth, one twelfth and one twelfth, which is one half.

The expectation is the balance point of the bars. Grouping equal probabilities makes the arithmetic one line: (1 + 2 + 5 + 7)/6 + (3 + 4 + 6 + 8)/12 = 15/6 + 21/12 = 2.5 + 1.75 = 4.25. It sits to the left of the midpoint 4.5 because the larger probabilities are on the smaller values.

The variance is the probability-weighted average of the squared distances from 4.25. It is 265/48, about 5.52, so the standard deviation is about 2.35. For contrast, spreading one eighth over each of the same eight values would give the mean 4.5 and the variance 63/12, which is 5.25: same support, different distribution, different answers.

Common misconception

Treating the eight values as equally likely because the question lists them. The unweighted average of 1 to 8 is 4.5, and the variance of the uniform distribution on those eight values is 5.25, but neither is the answer here: the probabilities are what carry the information. The second mistake is writing Var(X) = E[X^2] - E[X], which gives 283/12 - 51/12 = 19.33 instead of 5.52.

Quick check

For this table, what are E[X], F(4) and the standard deviation?

Answer

E[X] = 4.25, F(4) = 0.5 and the standard deviation is about 2.35. The mean is 51/12; the CDF at 4 adds the first four probabilities; and the variance 265/48 has square root 2.3496.
